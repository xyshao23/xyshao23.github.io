Research Profile

Tsinghua SIGS · Huawei 2012 Lab

I am a Ph.D. student in Electronic Information at Tsinghua University (SIGS), advised by Prof. Xiu Li. My research connects pretrained generative models with physical decision making: grounding language into actionable spatial representations, improving diffusion-based policies under limited demonstrations, and extracting compact task-conditioned priors from frozen video models.

Selected Publications

More than A Point: Modeling Spatial Uncertainty with Adaptive Affordance Heatmaps for Robotic Manipulation

Xinyu Shao, et al. CoRL 2026 · Accepted

Introduces adaptive affordance heatmaps for continuous target-region and spatial-uncertainty modeling. The data pipeline covers Open X-Embodiment trajectories; evaluation spans four grounding benchmarks and achieves 82% success in real-world manipulation.

arXivSpatial GroundingAffordanceUncertainty

LIP: A Single-Step Latent Interaction Prior from Frozen Video World Models for Robot Manipulation

Xinyu Shao, et al. ICRA 2027 · Under Review

Exposes the initial conditional response of a frozen flow-matching video model at the noise endpoint and compresses it into eight LIP tokens, without future-frame generation, action prediction, or video-backbone updates.

Video World ModelsFlow MatchingRobot Policy

MATS: Multi-Scale Action Transition Supervision for Diffusion Policy

Xinyu Shao, et al. ICASSP 2027 · Under Review

Adds multi-scale transition supervision to Diffusion Policy while preserving its architecture and inference procedure, improving LIBERO single-view success from 71.73% to 86.70%.

Diffusion PolicyAction ChunksTemporal Supervision

ResCue: Residual Spatial Cueing for Language-Conditioned Imitation Learning

Xinyu Shao, et al. ICASSP 2027 · Under Review

Injects independently encoded target masks into spatial RGB features through residual fusion. ResCue achieves 56.5% average success across 15 simulated tasks and 60.0% across three real-world bimanual tasks.

Language ConditioningImitation LearningSpatial Cueing

Generative Models for Physical Decision Making: A Unified Taxonomy

Xinyu Shao, et al. IEEE TNNLS · Under Review

Develops a unified taxonomy of generative modeling paradigms for imitation learning, reinforcement learning, robot planning, and closed-loop physical decision making.

Linear Differential Vision Transformer: Learning Visual Contrasts via Pairwise Differentials
