Xinyu Shao 邵新宇

Ph.D. Student @ Tsinghua University

I work on robot learning and embodied intelligence, with a focus on VLA policies, generative world models, and data-efficient robot manipulation.

I am a fourth-year direct-entry Ph.D. student in Electronic Information at Tsinghua University (SIGS), advised by Prof. Xiu Li in the Intelligent Computing Lab. My work connects pretrained generative models with physical decision making—grounding language into actionable spatial representations, improving diffusion-based policies under limited demonstrations, and extracting compact task-conditioned priors from frozen video models.

Research Interests

  • Vision-Language-Action Models
  • World Models for Robot Learning
  • Generative Policies / Diffusion Policies
  • Spatial Grounding & Affordance
  • Offline Reinforcement Learning and Sim-to-Real Transfer
