Xinyu Shao 邵新宇

Ph.D. Student @ Tsinghua University

I work on robot learning and embodied intelligence, with a focus on VLA policies, generative world models, and data-efficient robot manipulation.

I am a fourth-year direct-entry Ph.D. student in Electronic Information at Tsinghua University (SIGS), advised by Prof. Xiu Li in the Intelligent Computing Lab. I study how generative models support physical decision making through spatial grounding, data-efficient diffusion policies, and task-conditioned priors from frozen video models.

Research Interests

  • Vision-Language-Action Models
  • World Models for Robot Learning
  • Generative Policies / Diffusion Policies
  • Spatial Grounding & Affordance
